**Rayleigh momenta — what the port must reproduce.** This section is required, and it is the part
of `ORACLE.md` a port author most needs (source: `validation/RAYLEIGH-ZERO-FINDINGS.md` §8 and the
corrected `LEGACY-CODE-AUDIT.md` §1.3). State:

- A Rayleigh label is a device that keeps a non-rotationally-invariant denominator `1/|Q|²`
  symbolic; only `alpha`, `beta` and `InverseLaplacian` mint labels (plus the Wick-product fallback
  in `match_Wick_to_Rayleigh`), always in a denominator, and numerators are always written in the
  underlying momenta. `simplify_mul` throws on a matched positive power of a label.
- The reduction's integration order is forced — Rayleigh direction first (only ℓ = 0 survives),
  plane-wave direction second, loop direction last — and this is why only `FabJ(0, n, n)` arises.
  A port must keep this order or re-derive the reduction.
- Zero-valued rules (128 per run) are the normal signature of a `P13` self-contraction,
  `F₃(L, −L, k)`, and multiply identically-zero terms. Their cancellation needs
  `(L.i)²(L.j)⁻² = 1`, which GiNaC's indexed algebra does not perform; it happens at
  `dot_products_to_cos(K).expand()`, and the assertion `ERROR_RAYLEIGH_ZERO_LABEL_SURVIVES` sits
  immediately after that line. A port must reduce to scalar form before testing for zero, and must
  never substitute 0 into a label carrying a negative power (GiNaC: `power::eval(): division by
  zero`).
- Rayleigh values must be `±L ± k` with one external momentum; the kernel may carry at most two
  Legendre factors in the loop direction, at most one partnered to `k`; an unlabelled non-invariant
  denominator is *not* detected and produces either a GiNaC pole error or silently wrong output.
